FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the smallest amount I can borrow?

KES {{ min_amount|intcomma }}. The maximum is KES {{ max_amount|intcomma }}, and your limit grows as you repay on time.

Who can apply?

You need a Kenyan mobile number and a National ID. Identity review is manual. Submitting documents does not guarantee approval, and the service does not yet issue live loans while operating approvals are outstanding.

What does it cost to apply?

Nothing at registration. The processing fee shown above applies to an approved offer. In this environment fee collection is disabled, so no money is taken. If collection is enabled after approval, the fee is only requested after you accept the written offer.

Which documents do you ask for, and why?

The front and back of your National ID and a selfie, up to 5 MB each in JPEG, PNG or WebP. They are used to check that the person applying matches the identity record. Documents are optional when you create the account and can be uploaded from the dashboard.

How long does review take?

Review is performed by staff, not an automated score. Timing depends on document quality and volume. We do not promise a same-day decision, and the dashboard shows the actual state rather than a countdown.

How do I raise a concern?

Use the support channel published in the footer or the contact route in the privacy notice. Include your registered phone number and the request ID shown with any error message. We do not use fabricated response times or resolution guarantees.
