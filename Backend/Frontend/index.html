Privacy and security

Sensitive information deserves restraint.

These are controls in the application, not certification claims. External audits and regulatory approvals are still outstanding.

Private document storage

Identity images are written to a private bucket. The database stores storage paths, not public links. Reviewers receive signed links that expire after five minutes.

Minimum data on screen

Your National ID is masked in the dashboard, phone numbers are masked in logs, and private storage paths are never rendered into pages.

Server-authoritative money states

A browser callback never marks a payment successful. Charge outcomes require a signed provider webhook, a single stored event and an exact amount match.

You stay in control

Withdraw consent at any time, request your data, or ask for correction. Privacy requests use the route published in the notice.

Questions

Straight answers, including the limits.

Who can apply?

You need a Kenyan mobile number and a National ID. Identity review is manual. Submitting documents does not guarantee approval, and the service does not yet issue live loans while operating approvals are outstanding.

What does it cost to apply?

Nothing at registration. The processing fee shown above applies to an approved offer. In this environment fee collection is disabled, so no money is taken. If collection is enabled after approval, the fee is only requested after you accept the written offer.

Which documents do you ask for, and why?

The front and back of your National ID and a selfie, up to 5 MB each in JPEG, PNG or WebP. They are used to check that the person applying matches the identity record. Documents are optional when you create the account and can be uploaded from the dashboard.

How long does review take?

Review is performed by staff, not an automated score. Timing depends on document quality and volume. We do not promise a same-day decision, and the dashboard shows the actual state rather than a countdown.

Is my information safe?

Documents are kept in private storage and accessed through short-lived links. Access is recorded. The application does not claim bank-grade security, encryption certification or regulatory approval; those reviews remain outstanding.

What happens after I accept an offer?

If fee collection is enabled, you would pay the disclosed fee through the payment provider. Disbursement starts only after approval and payment confirmation, and is marked complete only when the provider confirms the transfer. This environment does not issue live credit, and repayment terms are not implemented.

How do I raise a concern?

Use the support channel published in the footer or the contact route in the privacy notice. Include your registered phone number and the request ID shown with any error message. We do not use fabricated response times or resolution guarantees.
