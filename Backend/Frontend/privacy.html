Privacy notice

This notice explains what we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it and how to exercise your rights.

Draft — version {{ PRIVACY_VERSION }}. This notice has not been approved for live lending. It describes the controls implemented in the application; it is not a regulatory certification or a substitute for legal advice.

Licence status: {{ PLATFORM_LICENCE_STATUS }}.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: mobile number, National ID number, password hash and consent records.
  • Identity documents: front and back of your National ID and a selfie, if you upload them.
  • Application data: requested amount, disbursement phone number, status history and staff decisions.
  • Payment data: provider references, amounts, ledger entries and reconciliation results. We do not store card numbers.
  • Technical data: request IDs, IP address, user agent, and security audit events such as document-access logs.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and to record the consent you give.
  • To verify identity and review an application, which happens manually.
  • To prepare an offer, process an approved fee and confirm a disbursement through the payment provider.
  • To prevent fraud, investigate disputes, meet legal obligations and maintain audit records.

Identity documents are processed on the basis of your explicit consent. Account and application data are processed to take steps you request before entering an agreement and to operate the service. Where the law requires a different basis, that basis will be documented before live operations.

4. Who can see it

Access is limited to authorised staff who review applications and to service providers that host the database, store documents or process payments. Providers act under contract and only for the purposes described here. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

Current providers: Supabase (database and private object storage), Paystack (payments), and an SMS provider only when messaging is configured. Processor agreements and region positions are an outstanding launch gate.

5. How long we keep it

Identity images are retained while an application is reviewed and for a limited period afterwards. The retention job is configured for {{ DOCUMENT_RETENTION_DAYS }} days. Financial and audit records may be kept longer where the law requires it. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

6. How we protect it

  • Documents are written to a private bucket; the database stores storage paths rather than public links.
  • Reviewers receive signed links that expire after five minutes, and every generated link is recorded.
  • Phone numbers are masked in routine logs, and National IDs are masked on screen.
  • Administrative access is restricted to staff accounts. Two-factor authentication is not yet implemented and is a launch blocker.

We do not claim bank-grade security, encryption certification or regulatory approval. Independent security testing is outstanding.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent for future identity processing; this does not affect processing already carried out.

Use the support channel published in the footer, or the privacy contact once it is published. Include your registered phone number and the request. We will verify your identity before acting on a request. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

8. Children

This service is not intended for anyone under 18. Do not submit identity documents for a child.

When this notice changes, the version shown above changes and you may be asked to record consent again before applying. The current version is {{ PRIVACY_VERSION }}.
