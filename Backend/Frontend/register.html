What we ask for, and why.

  • Mobile number — identifies your account and receives status messages.
  • National ID — matched against the documents you upload.
  • Password — protects your account. We never display or email it.

Identity documents are optional at registration. You can upload them from your dashboard when you are ready; review is manual.

No payment is collected at registration. The fee schedule is published on the homepage, and fee collection is disabled while the required approvals are outstanding.
